## 🛠️ Complete Directory of 24 PDF Tools

### 📂 Organize & Optimize
1. **Merge PDF**: Combine multiple PDF files into a single document in your preferred order.
2. **Split PDF**: Separate specific pages or split large documents into individual files.
3. **Compress PDF**: Shrink file sizes significantly without losing text or image clarity.
4. **Organize PDF**: Rearrange, delete, or rotate pages within your PDF document seamlessly.
5. **Crop PDF**: Trim the margins and customize the visible canvas area of your pages.

### 🔄 Convert TO PDF (Any File to PDF)
6. **Word to PDF**: Convert DOCX and DOC files into clean, professional PDFs.
7. **Excel to PDF**: Turn XLS and XLSX spreadsheets into neatly formatted PDF tables.
8. **PPT to PDF**: Transform PowerPoint presentations (PPTX) into easy-to-share PDF slides.
9. **JPG to PDF**: Convert JPG/JPEG images into high-quality PDF files in seconds.
10. **PNG to PDF**: Save transparent or high-res PNG images directly into a PDF format.
11. **HTML to PDF**: Render web pages into offline readable PDF documents via URL.



### ✍️ Edit & Enhance

12. **Sign PDF**: Create, upload, or draw your electronic signature to sign documents legally.
13. **Add Page Numbers**: Number your PDF pages automatically with customizable fonts, sizes, and positions.
14. **Watermark PDF**: Stamp text or an image overlay onto your document to protect your copyright.

### 🔒 Security & Privacy
15. **Protect PDF**: Encrypt your sensitive files with strong, industry-standard passwords.
16. **Unlock PDF**: Remove passwords and restrictions from secured PDFs (if you own the rights).
17. **Repair PDF**: Fix and recover data from corrupted, broken, or damaged PDF files.
